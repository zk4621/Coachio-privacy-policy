Delete your Coachio account

Coachio, developed by Ziga Kovacic — Last updated: 2026-08-13

Delete it yourself, in the app

This is the fastest route and takes effect immediately.

  1. Open Coachio and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account and the data listed below are erased as soon as you confirm. The action cannot be undone, so export anything you still need first.

Or ask us to delete it

If you can no longer sign in — or you have already removed the app — email ziga.kovacic777@gmail.com from the address you registered with, with the subject “Coachio account deletion request”.

Sending it from the registered address is how we confirm the request is yours; if you cannot, we will ask you for something else that proves it before deleting anything. Requests are handled within 30 days, normally much sooner.

What is deleted

Everything tied to your account, including:

Data What happens
Account details — name, email address, sign-in credentials Deleted
Your players — names, email addresses, phone numbers, notes, levels Deleted
Sessions, courts, attendance and session notes Deleted
Invoices, payment status, hour packages and earnings history Deleted
Tournaments and their expenses Deleted
App settings and preferences Deleted

What is kept, and for how long

  • Voice recordings: nothing to delete. Audio you dictate is sent to our speech-to-text provider to be transcribed and is not stored by Coachio. Only the resulting session — which is deleted with your account — is kept.
  • Diagnostic and crash logs: up to 12 months. These are kept for stability and abuse prevention and are not linked to your name or email once the account is gone.
  • Records we are legally required to keep. Where accounting or tax law obliges us to retain a record, we keep only that record, only for the period the law requires, and use it for nothing else.
  • Calendar events already written to your own calendar. If you connected Google Calendar, sessions Coachio created live in your calendar account and stay under your control — delete them there if you want them gone.
