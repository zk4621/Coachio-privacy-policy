Privacy Policy

For Coachio — Last updated: 2026-08-13

1) Controller

Ziga Kovacic, Slovenia. Contact: ziga.kovacic777@gmail.com

2) What We Collect

  • Account data: name, email address, and sign-in credentials.
  • People you coach: the names, email addresses, phone numbers, skill levels and notes you enter about your players. You enter this about other people, so see “Data about your players” below.
  • Sessions: dates, times, courts, attendance and your session notes.
  • Billing: rates, amounts, invoice status and hour packages. No card details are stored by the app.
  • Voice recordings: if you use voice to create or edit a session, the audio is sent to a speech-to-text provider to be transcribed. Coachio does not store the recording — only the resulting session.
  • Technical data: device model, app version, diagnostic and crash logs, and anonymous usage events.

3) How We Use Data

  • Manage schedules and confirm session attendance.
  • Calculate monthly costs per player and send invoices/notifications via email.
  • Provide user support and improve the app.
  • Comply with legal obligations (e.g., accounting).

4) Legal Bases (GDPR)

  • Contract performance (providing the app’s core features).
  • Legitimate interests (abuse prevention, app improvements).
  • Consent (e.g., optional notifications/marketing, where applicable).

5) Sharing

We share data with service providers only as necessary to operate the app. We do not sell personal data.

  • Backend database and auth: Supabase (hosting, authentication, database, storage). This is where your account and coaching data live.
  • Speech-to-text: OpenAI and, where selected, Soniox. When you dictate a session, the audio and the names of your players and courts are sent to be transcribed and turned into a draft session. These providers process data outside the EU.
  • Google Calendar: only if you connect it. Sessions you create are then written to your own Google Calendar, under your Google account.
  • Apple Calendar: only if you enable it on iOS. Events are written on the device itself and are not sent to us.
  • Crash reporting and product analytics: Sentry (crash diagnostics) and PostHog (anonymous usage events, EU-hosted).
  • Payments: Stripe, where you choose to collect a player’s payment through it. Card data is handled by Stripe and is never stored in the app.
  • Email/communications: the email service configured for sending invoices and notifications.

6) Data Retention

  • We retain data while a user maintains an account or as required by law.
  • Logs and analytics: up to 12 months (unless a shorter period is configured).

7) Security

  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Access controls and standard security practices. Supabase provides security features such as Row Level Security (RLS) which we configure for least‑privilege access.

7b) Data about your players

Most of what you keep in Coachio is about other people — the players you coach. They are not Coachio users and have not agreed to anything with us, so the responsibility for that data is yours: under GDPR you are the controller for it and we process it on your behalf.

In practice that means:

  • Enter only what you actually need to run your coaching.
  • Tell your players that you keep their details in an app, and be ready to show or correct what you hold if they ask.
  • Deleting a player, or your whole account, removes their details from our systems too.

We do not contact your players, market to them, or use their details for anything beyond running the app for you.

7c) Deleting your account

You can delete your account and its data at any time from Profile → Settings → Delete account in the app, which takes effect immediately, or by emailing us.

Full instructions, and exactly what is deleted and what is briefly retained, are on the account deletion page.
